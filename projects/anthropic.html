About

As an Anthropic Ambassador, I founded an organization at Illinois Tech to close the gap between AI capability and AI literacy among the next generation of builders. In partnership with Anthropic, the club hosts workshops, hackathons, and learning sessions designed to give students hands-on experience with AI tools, responsible AI practice, and the research thinking behind modern language models.

The student kickoff was one of the largest AI-focused student events in Anthropic’s recent history. What followed was a series of sessions spanning prompt engineering, AI product thinking, mechanistic interpretability concepts, and applied project work. All were designed around one premise: that students who understand how AI works are better positioned to shape how it gets used.
